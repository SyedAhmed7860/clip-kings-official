Top Men's Hairstyles for 2026

The Return to Texture

2026 is seeing a massive shift away from hyper-rigid, heavily gelled styles and a strong return to natural texture and flow. Men want hair that looks effortlessly perfect but still maintains a sharp, clean perimeter.

The Textured Crop

Paired with a blunt fringe and a high, tight skin fade, this low-maintenance style is dominating. It's versatile, aggressive, and stylish. Use a matte clay or texturizing powder to achieve that choppy, separated look on top without looking greasy.

The Modern Mullet & The Executive Contour

The mullet is still here, but it has evolved. It now features less party in the back and more sophistication all around, with clean burst fades on the sides and heavy texture. On the other end of the spectrum, the Executive Contour remains a timeless classic. A razor-sharp side part with a tight taper, styled with a high-shine pomade, is the ultimate power move for the corporate kings.
